In the fall of 2021, math teacher Kristen Smith returned to the classroom after 18 months of virtual teaching to ninth graders who had resigned themselves to not being able to learn math. Many of them hadn't been able to attend virtual school consistently, and the gaps in their understanding of middle school standards were obvious right away. She was struck by how many students blamed themselves for those gaps .
